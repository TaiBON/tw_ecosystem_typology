# S2 地下人造洞穴生物群系

地下礦坑隧道。

地下人造洞穴生物群系是歸因於人類挖掘行為形成的單一功能生態群系。 出現於地下礦坑、運輸隧道、墓穴、防禦和能源設施和其他基礎建設。 大多是於工業時代用土方機械建構而成的，部分是數千年前手工建造。 生產力低，能量通常來自地表大氣擴散或滲漏而來，部分能量是由化學自營微生物提供。 雖然沒有日照或光線高度漫射，但一些活動洞穴有人工照明，提供充足能量來維持藻類自營。 營養網主要由伺機性微生物和無脊椎動物組成，透過機械、人類直接引入或自行由洞穴開口進入。 後者包括利用洞穴作為庇護所或繁殖地的小型哺乳動物。 外來及原生的微生物迅速定植於新暴露的岩石表面，並形成生物膜供給食碎屑動物並加速基質風化。 地下人造洞穴的穩定性受基質和管理方式而變化，部分洞穴廢棄後容易發生坍塌和結構變化。

# S2.1 地下人造洞穴

群系: 地下人造洞穴
生態域: 地下

## 生態特徵

透過挖掘形成的充氣地下洞穴生產力較低。 儘管與富氧洞穴（S1.1）相似，此生態系的結構更為簡單、歷史較短、生物多樣性較低、演化譜系較少且無特有種。 由於近期人類開發造成奠基者效應（物種遷移少，演化分歧時間也少），並且相對於天然洞穴，人造洞穴結構簡單使微棲地生境多樣性低，導致物種多樣性低、特有性低以及機會性生物性狀。 營養網絡由異營生物主導，通常沒有優勢光合初級生產者或食草動物。 Generalist detritivores and their pathogens and predators dominate, although some specialists may be associated with bat dung deposits. Biota include invertebrates (notably beetles, springtails and arachnids), fungi, bacteria and transient vertebrates, notably bats, which use the voids as roosts and breeding sites. Bacteria and fungi form biofilms on void surfaces. Many are colonists of human inoculations, with some microbes identified as ‘human- indicator bacteria’ (e.g. Escherichia coli, Staphylococcus aureus and high-temperature Bacillus spp.). Fungi are most abundant in humid microsites. Some are parasites and many are critical food sources for invertebrates and protozoans. Sources of energy and nutrients are allochthonous, imported by humans, bats, winged invertebrates, other animals, and seepage moisture. Many taxa have long life spans, slow metabolism and growth and low fecundity, but lack distinctive traits found in the biota of natural caves. Some are temporary below-ground inhabitants, have populations that live entirely above- or below-ground, or have life cycles necessitating the use of both environments.

## 關鍵生態驅動力

Excavations associated with tunnels, vaults and mines. While some are abandoned, others are continuously accessed by humans, enhancing connectivity with the surface, resource importation and biotic dispersal. Substrates include a range of rock types as well as artificial surfaces on linings and debris piles. Air movement varies
from still to turbulent (i.e. active train tunnels). Light is absent except at openings and where artificial sources are maintained by humans, sometimes supporting algae (i.e. lampenflora). Humidity and temperature are relatively constant, and nutrients are scarce except where enriched by human sources.

## DISTRIBUTION

Scattered worldwide, but mostly associated with urban centres, transit corridors and industrial mines.
